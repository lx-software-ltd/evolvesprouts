# Plan: ESLint 10 ESM configs

**Status**: Done
**Zone**: yellow

## Goal

Run Admin Web, the public website, and the training site on ESLint 10 with ESM configs. Keep `@eslint/compat` `fixupConfigRules` until `eslint-plugin-react` declares ESLint 10 support. Issue 402 stays open.

## Non-goals

- No Next.js or `eslint-config-next` version bump.
- No removal of the compat wrapper. Four react spacing rules crash on ESLint 10 without it.
- No change to `backend/infrastructure`.
- No `"type": "module"` and no `next.config.mjs` conversion.
- No change in `lx-software-ltd/actions`. `setup-node` already defaults to Node 26.

## Invariants this change preserves

- Custom lint rules, ignores, and the `espree` parser block for JS-family files stay.
- `fixupConfigRules` stays around `eslint-config-next`.
- `espree` is a direct devDependency at `^11.2.0`, the range ESLint 10 depends on.
- `next.config.js` stays CommonJS.
- Dependabot still ignores later ESLint, `eslint-config-next`, `@eslint/compat`, and `typescript-eslint` majors.
- TypeScript 7 and `deepmerge` Dependabot ignores stay.
- Other package overrides stay.
- Lockfiles keep main's versions outside the ESLint 10 tree.
