### What the navigation data says

- Tap-forward on a 60s story is expected; most story frames are 5–15s and
  viewers are trained to tap. The number to worry about is **swipe
  forward at 19%** — those 22 viewers did not just skip the frame, they
  left `@evolvesprouts` for the next account. On the image story the same
  metric is 6.5%.
- Combined exit rate (tap exit + swipe forward) is **28.7%**, versus 15.2%
  on the image story. A single long frame concentrates all the drop-off
  in one place, so there is no way to see *where* in the 60s people left.
- The frame strip shows the hook problem: the first ~20s is the product
  image with the presenter in a small picture-in-picture and the line
  "asked me what I think about this toy". The answer ("not bad, but...")
  and the proof shot (real knife, real banana, "look at what actually
  cutting is") arrive after most tappers have gone.
- Captions are small and sit in the lower third, overlapping the
  reply bar on most phones. Most story views are muted, so legibility is
  retention.

## Recommendations

Ordered by expected impact against effort. Items 1–5 are about the next
story set; 6–10 are the account-level changes the story data points to.

### Stories

1. **Cut long talking-heads into 3–4 frames of ≤15s each.** Frame 1:
   the proof shot (real knife, real banana) with the verdict as a large
   text overlay — "Velcro pizza: fine, but it teaches the hand nothing".
   Frame 2: why. Frame 3: what to do instead. Frame 4: poll or question.
   This also gives per-frame drop-off, which a single 60s clip cannot.
2. **Put a sticker on every story.** Poll on frame 1 ("Got the velcro
   pizza? Yes / No"), question box on the last frame ("Which toy should I
   review next?"), and a **link sticker** to the relevant page tagged
   `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=toy-review&utm_content=velcro-pizza`
   (per the UTM convention in the README). Replies were 1 and website taps
   are 10 a month; stickers are the only lever inside Stories that moves
   either number.
3. **Add a one-line take to every reshare, or do not reshare.** The
   @zerotothree frame held attention well (6.5% swipe-away) but had
   nothing to reply to and nothing of the brand in it. A text overlay
   like "Also true at 2 years old — ask any Hong Kong mum on a Saturday"
   and a poll converts a borrowed laugh into an owned interaction.
4. **Post stories daily, in a fixed rhythm.** Two or three frames a day:
   one real-session or behind-the-scenes clip, one tip or reshare of an
   own post, one prompt (poll, question, "DM me"). Stories are the only
   format reliably reaching existing followers, and 42% of followers saw
   nothing in 30 days. Target: follower reach from 58% to 80%+ by the next
   monthly pull.
5. **Captions: bigger, higher, auto-generated.** Keep text inside the
   centre safe zone (clear of the top 250px and bottom 300px), use
   Instagram's caption sticker so it renders at readable size, and lead
   each frame with the key phrase in the first line.

### Measurement for stories

- Run `python3 scripts/instagram-assessment.py --out generated-reports/$(date -u +%F)-instagram.txt`
  within 24 hours of each story set (the Monday pull in the README is
  not often enough, since story insights expire). Track per-story
  **reach % of followers**, **swipe-forward rate**, **replies + shares**,
  and **link taps** once a link sticker is in use.
- Timing: the Tuesday 21:41 HKT post performed well. `online_followers`
  is not populated for this account via the API yet; check Insights →
  Total followers → Most active times in the app and test 07:30–08:30 HKT
  against 20:30–21:30 HKT over the next two weeks.
- Save every reviewed toy into a **"Worth it?" highlight** and every
  session clip into a **"Real sessions" highlight** so story effort
  outlives 24 hours and feeds profile visitors (182 a month) something to
  watch.

### Account-level

6. **Restore feed cadence to two posts a week.** Daily reach fell ~30%
   through October with one post in three weeks. July's cadence of seven
   posts in four weeks produced 106–362 reach per post with no ad support.
7. **Cut Reels to 20–30 seconds.** Average watch time is 16.7–18.9s on
   60s Reels. Open on the proof shot, put the verdict in the first
   sentence, and let the caption carry the nuance.
8. **Lean into what already converts.** The Aug 25 "real session in a HK
   flat" carousel is the only recent post with saves, shares, profile
   visits and a follow together. One real-session post a week (with
   consent and no identifying details) is the strongest organic lead-in
   to consultations. Personal HK-anchored narratives (Sep 22 Reel) are the
   strongest reach format.
9. **Fix the follow ratio.** 680 following against 405 followers reads
   as follow-for-follow to a profile visitor deciding whether to follow.
   Unfollow inactive or irrelevant accounts until following is below the
   follower count.
10. **Make the bio link earn its taps.** `/links` is the destination for
    10 taps a month. Point the bio and story link stickers at the specific
    offer the content supports (course page or consultations page) with
    UTMs, so GA4's `sessionCampaignName` shows Instagram organic
    separately from paid social.

## Data Integrity Notes

- Instagram Graph API queried via system user token against the IG
  business account ID in `EVOLVESPROUTS_META_INSTAGRAM_BUSINESS_ACCOUNT_ID`.
  All calls read-only.
- Requests target `v21.0`; the API auto-upgraded paging links to `v25.0`
  and rejects `impressions` for stories ("no longer supported from
  v22.0"), so this report uses `views` and `reach` only.
- Story navigation breakdown does not include automatic advance to the
  next frame or to the next account after the frame ends, so navigation
  totals (95 and 30) are lower than views (115 and 46).
- Account-level reach by content type and by follow type is a sum of
  daily unique values; an account reached on multiple days counts more
  than once, so the breakdown total (3,572) exceeds the window total
  (3,182).
- `online_followers` returned an empty value for the account.
- The image story was 7.6 hours old at pull time, so its totals are not
  final and are not directly comparable with the 21-hour-old video.
